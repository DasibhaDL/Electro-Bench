Privacy Policy for Electro Bench

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains how the Electro Bench app for Android ("the app"), published by DasibhaDL ("we", "us"), handles information. The app has no user accounts, and we do not run any servers that receive your data.

What stays on your phone

The following are stored only on your device and are never sent to us:

- Searches, history and saved parts. The marking codes and part numbers you look up, and the parts you save.
- Repair notebook. The jobs you create: device names, customer or ticket notes, faults, parts and photos you add.
- Settings. Your theme and quick-tool choices.

The marking-code and pinout libraries are built into the app, so looking up a part needs no internet connection.

You can delete this information at any time: remove entries or jobs inside the app, or clear the app's storage (Settings → Apps → Electro Bench → Storage → Clear data), or uninstall the app.

Camera and photos

The app uses your camera, or a photo you choose, only when you ask it to:

- Scanning a marking code. The image is read on your phone using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. The image is not uploaded or stored.
- Photos in the repair notebook. Photos you add to a job are kept on your phone with that job.

Sharing

When you share a pinout, a result or a repair job, the app creates an image or text and hands it to the app you pick in Android's share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- your device's advertising ID
- approximate location, derived from your IP address
- information about the app and your device, such as the device model, operating system and app version
- ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped
- crash logs and diagnostic data from the ads SDK

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is sent over an encrypted connection. Google's use of it is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices:

- Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under About → Privacy settings in the app.
- Everyone can reset or delete their advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your phone).

Links to other sites

Some buttons open a web search for a part's datasheet, or the app's Play Store page. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is intended for general audiences working with electronics and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
